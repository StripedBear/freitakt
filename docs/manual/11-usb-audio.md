# USB audio
<!--blurb: Record the stereo master into a DAW over USB.-->

## Overview

The FM-1 is a USB audio input as well as a MIDI port: a DAW, AUM or Audio MIDI Setup records it over the cable, 16-bit stereo, 44.1 kHz. The device is called **Hortator**, and it carries the stereo master, exactly as the speaker and headphones hear it.

There is nothing to choose in the menu: USB audio is always stereo. (FREITAKT removed the older USB AUDIO 10 CH mode, which sent the tracks on their own channels; a 10 CH setting saved by an older firmware is ignored.)

## Level

MENU > **USB LEVEL** picks what the computer gets: **MASTER** follows the MASTER knob, as the speaker does; **FIXED** sends the full level whatever MASTER is set to, and MASTER then sets only the speaker and headphones.

## Computers and limits

- **macOS, iPadOS, Linux and Windows** take it (class-compliant USB audio 1.0, no driver to install).
- A **safe start** presents the same stereo device; the installers and the editor find the FM-1 by this name.
